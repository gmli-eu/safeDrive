safeDrive

When you can't call for help, your car does.

Team madpiece · Junction 2026

A crash, a medical emergency or a threatening passenger can leave a driver unable to reach their phone. safeDrive makes the car raise the alarm by itself: a hidden SOS button, a crash or rollover, or a threat spotted by the cabin camera sends the driver's contacts a live location, a photo, a video clip and an AI incident report within seconds. Inside the car, an offline voice assistant, a back-seat translator and a camera that notices closed eyes or a phone in hand keep the driver's eyes on the road.
